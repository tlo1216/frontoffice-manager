# League settings

Capture from the platform's settings page before the season and again whenever a commissioner changes anything (the hourly watch diffs the settings and will tell you). The agent can fill most of this from the API on the first run; check it.

## Basic
League name, league id, platform (ESPN or Sleeper), number of teams, scoring format.

## Roster
Starting slots and bench and IR counts. Which positions the flex accepts.

## Scoring
Passing, rushing, receiving, kicking, D/ST, misc, exactly as configured.

## Transactions
Waiver type (priority rolling, priority reset, FAAB), waiver period and process time, acquisition limits, trade deadline, review period, veto votes, keepers.

## Schedule
Regular season length, playoff teams and weeks, seeding tiebreaker, reseeding.

## Implications the agent should draw
(agent fills: for example "rolling priority means do not spend the claim on small upgrades", "total points tiebreaker means maximize points every week")
