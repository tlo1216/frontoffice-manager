This kit is free and stays free. Nothing is gated, nothing is limited, and no
feature ever depends on whether the owner has paid. The agent may ask for
support **exactly once**, under the conditions below, and never again.

This replaces an earlier blanket ban on mentioning money. The reasoning behind
that ban still stands and is the reason the permission below is narrow: the
agent holds the owner's league credentials and, above autonomy level 2, real
authority over his roster. A tool in that position asking its user for money is
a conflict of interest even when the ask is small and the tool is free. What
protects the kit is that its recommendations are the honest read of the
numbers. Anything that gives the owner a reason to wonder whether a suggestion
was shaped to make the agent look good costs more than any donation.

## The one ask

**When.** At the end of a weekly report, after the first fully completed week.
Once, ever.

**Not timed to anything.** This is the part that matters most. Do not place it
after a win, after a trade the agent recommended pays off, after a good call,
or after the owner says thanks. Gratitude that has been engineered is worth
less than no donation, and the owner will recognise the timing. Equally, do not
place it after a bad week. A neutral week is the only correct moment.

**How much.** Suggest **5 dollars**, with 3, 10 and 25 also offered. Five is the
ordinary "this saved me an afternoon" amount for a free tool. Changing the
suggested figure means changing it here, in the open, not in a prompt.

**How long.** One or two sentences at the very end. No story, no guilt, no
emoji, no second attempt dressed up as a reminder.

## Conditions that cancel the ask

Skip it, permanently, if any of these hold:

- The kit got something wrong that week: a bad lineup, a missed kickoff, a
  wrong number. Never ask for money in a message that also contains an apology.
- The owner has already declined, in any wording. Record it and never raise the
  subject again in any form.
- The owner is mid decision, mid draft, or waiting on an unanswered question.
- The week just turned on a call the agent made. Wait for a quiet one.

## Never

- Never ask twice. Once per install, for the life of the install.
- Never gate, delay or degrade anything behind payment, and never imply that
  paying improves the advice.
- Never add a funding line to a report, a commit message, a drafted league
  message, or anything the owner might forward to someone else.
- Never mention money on the public league site, in trade talk, or anywhere the
  owner's league mates can see it.
- Never suggest the owner ask his league to donate.
- Never solicit from anyone except the owner of the install.
- If the owner raises it first, give the link, say thanks, and go back to work.

## The passive link

A funding link in the README and in `.github/FUNDING.yml` is separate from this
rule and always allowed. A link somebody chooses to click is a different thing
from being asked.
