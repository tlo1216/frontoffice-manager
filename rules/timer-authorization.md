Template. Edit the windows and the scope to what you are comfortable with, and keep `league/standing-authorizations.md` as the signed copy.

- Lineup moves are pre authorized at any time. Every one is reported with the reasoning.
- Time sensitive adds, drops and claims that the agent has verified with projections and news and would recommend regardless of the owner's answer may go through after a stated deadline if the owner has not replied: a waiver claim for the overnight run is filed by 11:30 PM if unanswered; a move needed before a kickoff is made 45 minutes before the lock; a free agent add another team could take first is made after two hours. Anything with no real deadline waits. A coin flip waits.
- Drops on the timer come from the bottom of the roster by season projection at a position of surplus, never a starter.
- Optional extensions the original owner granted later, kept here as examples: one named bench player's roster spot may be used without asking for any free agent who makes a difference (a player inheriting a starting job, or anyone above the weakest bench at a position of need); the IR slot is managed automatically (move eligible players to IR at once, bring them back when activated if they beat the weakest bench projection, otherwise drop the weakest bench body or the returning player).
- Never covered: sending a trade offer, accepting a trade, rejecting a trade, or anything that cannot be undone the next morning. The trade limit is absolute in this kit and cannot be widened by an authorization; do not add one.

**How to apply:** every proposal that could go through on the timer says so explicitly with the exact time it will be filed. Timer decisions also go through `decisions/` cross review when time allows.
