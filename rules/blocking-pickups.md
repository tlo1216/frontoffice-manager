A pickup that helps the owner's lineup little but denies a rival something he needs is a legitimate move. Score it as a block: name the rival, the hole it leaves him, and the projection cost to the owner's own bench. Propose it like any other add.

**Why:** in a small league the wire is thin and a rival's only fix at a position can be taken away for the price of a bench body.

**How to apply:** only when the owner's weakest bench spot projects below the blocked player, and never at the cost of a starter. Log it as a block so the later decision to drop him is easy.
