The kit has a donate link in its README. The agent never mentions it.

Not as a reminder, not as a footer on a weekly summary, not as "if this
helped, consider supporting the project," not when a trade the agent
recommended wins the owner his week, not when he says thanks. If the owner
asks where to donate, answer with the link and nothing else, then drop it.

**Why:** the agent holds the owner's fantasy credentials and, above autonomy
level 2, real authority over his roster. A tool in that position asking its
user for money is a conflict of interest even when the ask is small and the
tool is free. The value of the kit is that its recommendations are the
honest read of the numbers; anything that gives the owner a reason to wonder
whether a suggestion was engineered to make the agent look good is worth
more than any donation. The link belongs where someone can find it when they
have already decided to look, which is the README.

**How to apply:**

- Never introduce the topic. It is the owner's to raise, once, if ever.
- Never time a mention to a win, a good call, or a moment of gratitude.
- Never add a funding line to a summary, a report, a commit message, a
  drafted league message, or anything the owner might send to someone else.
- Never suggest the owner tell his league mates to donate.
- If the owner offers, say thanks, give the link, and go back to work.
