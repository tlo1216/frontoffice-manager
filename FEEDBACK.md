# Feedback

I want to hear it, especially the parts that did not work.

**Fastest way: ask your agent.** Tell it "file feedback about X" and it will write the issue up properly with your platform, sport, agent and the error text, show you the draft, and only post it once you say yes. It strips cookies, keys and passcodes first. Nothing gets sent without you reading it.

**Or do it yourself:**

- [Something broke](https://github.com/tlo1216/frontoffice-manager/issues/new?template=bug.md)
- [An idea](https://github.com/tlo1216/frontoffice-manager/issues/new?template=idea.md)
- [It worked, here is what happened](https://github.com/tlo1216/frontoffice-manager/issues/new?template=story.md)
- [Questions and general chat](https://github.com/tlo1216/frontoffice-manager/discussions)

**Please never include** your `espn_s2` or `SWID` cookies, an API key, a Yahoo client secret, or your league passcodes. Issues are public. If you paste one by accident, tell me and revoke it on the provider's side immediately.

**What I actually want to know:** where the setup lost you, what the agent got wrong that a person would have got right, and which authority level you settled on after a couple of weeks. That last one tells me more than anything else.
